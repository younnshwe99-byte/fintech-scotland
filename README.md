Investment readiness score

Investment readiness was operationalised using the Payne scorecard dimensions and weights set out in Table 1. For each firm, the text disclosed on its FinTech Scotland directory profile [https://www.fintechscotland.com/cluster/fintech-community/] comprising the company overview, company story and team biographies — was assessed against each of the six dimensions and awarded a score between 0 and 100, where 0 indicates no positive signal and 100 an exceptionally strong and specific signal. Dimension scores were then multiplied by their respective weights and summed to produce a composite score out of 100.
Assessment was carried out using a large language model, applied through a standardised rubric specifying, for each dimension, the disclosure types that warranted higher and lower scores. Every firm was assessed using an identical rubric and prompt structure, with only the firm-specific text varying between cases, ensuring consistent application of the criteria across the sample. Three decision rules were embedded in the rubric: absence of evidence was scored low or neutral rather than assumed positive; no credit was awarded for characteristics the text did not state; and unsupported promotional language did not raise a score. The model was additionally required to return a one-sentence justification for each dimension score, referencing the specific disclosure that drove it, allowing every score to be audited against the underlying text. The full rubric as administered is reproduced at Appendix A, and the complete set of dimension scores and justifications is available in the accompanying dataset.


Regulatory sandbox participation

Participation in the FCA Regulatory Sandbox was recorded as a binary indicator. Firms accepted into the sandbox are published by the FCA by cohort; the full set of participating firms was extracted from the FCA website [https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/regulatory-sandbox] and matched against the sample by company name. Matches were verified manually to account for trading names, subsequent rebranding and participation by a parent or subsidiary entity, and a firm was coded as a participant where any such match could be confirmed.

Three firms in the sample were identified as sandbox participants. Given this number, sandbox participation is reported descriptively and no inferential claims are drawn from it; the finding of interest is the low absolute level of participation rather than any difference between participants and non-participants.

Bank partnership

Bank partnership was recorded as a binary indicator derived from the same disclosed profile text used for the readiness assessment. To examine the role of bank–fintech relationships in Scotland’s fintech ecosystem, firms were classified based on evidence of engagement with banks, including partnerships, collaborations, integrations, or provision of services to banking institutions. This captures commercial connections between fintech firms and the banking sector. Firms were classified as bank-linked where direct evidence of such relationships existed. Investment readiness scores and funding-stage distributions were then compared between bank-linked and non-bank-linked firms to examine differences in investment attractiveness and financing progression between bank-linked and non-bank-linked firms.
